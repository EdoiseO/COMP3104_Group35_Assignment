The Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) is the process used to plan, design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain software.

The main SDLC stages include planning, requirements analysis, design, development, testing, deployment, and maintenance. Each stage contributes to building software that meets user needs.

DevOps improves the SDLC by encouraging developers and operations teams to communicate and collaborate throughout the software process. This can reduce delays and help teams identify problems earlier.
